# Product

## What it does

1. **Finds jobs.** AI engineering and agent engineering roles that are US-remote or in the
   Research Triangle area of North Carolina.
2. **Rates fit.** Scores each job against the candidate's profile, with reasons and unmet
   requirements.
3. **Tailors the resume.** Rewrites the resume around the posting's exact words (its
   qualifications, action verbs, and tech stack), as a one-page Word document per job.
   Every bullet is backed by the candidate's own documents.
4. **Answers the application.** Prepares an answer for every question on the form,
   including "why our company", in the candidate's voice from their writing samples.
5. **Shows it all in one place.** The dashboard lists each job with its score and a link to
   the role on the company's site when possible. The Apply sidebar shows every answer
   with a Copy button and the resume to download.

## Who it is for

One candidate, who is both the buyer and the user. See `customers/`.

## The promise

Convenience: the candidate spends their time choosing jobs and reviewing answers, not
searching, tailoring, or drafting.

## Principles

- Private by default: the candidate's data stays on their machine and out of Git.
- Grounded: no invented facts, skills, or numbers.
- The candidate decides: nothing is submitted without them.
